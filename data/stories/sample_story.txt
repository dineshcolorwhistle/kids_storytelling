Once upon a time, in a bright green valley, lived a cheerful little rabbit named Barnaby. Barnaby loved to hop through the meadow and look for sweet wild clover. One sunny morning, he noticed a tiny blue butterfly resting on a golden dandelion. "Good morning, little flutterby!" Barnaby giggled. Together, they danced all the way to the whispering brook, where the water sparkled like diamonds. It was the happiest day in the whole forest.
